PLAIN-ENGLISH GUIDE

What am I looking at?

This demo shows how a normal shopping application hands slow or unreliable work to a background engine. Here is the whole vocabulary in one place.

OrderWhat the customer wants. It is saved first, then the background work begins.
JobA work ticket for the engine, such as “charge this payment” or “pack this parcel”.
QueueA waiting line. HIGH-priority tickets are checked more often than LOW-priority tickets.
WorkerA slot that actually runs the job. If all workers are busy, work waits safely in the queue.
RetryA second chance after a temporary failure, such as a payment gateway being slow.
Dead letterA job that used all of its retries. A person can replay it or discard it.
Idempotency keyA unique label that prevents the same action from being accepted twice.
LeaseA time-limited claim on a job. If a worker disappears, the lease expires and the job can be recovered.
